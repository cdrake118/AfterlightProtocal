How the Game Works

Investigators

  • Search with flashlights and drain Anomaly Health to 0.
  • Manage battery economy and revive teammates by proximity.
  • Use warning cues and map knowledge to predict ghost movement.

Anomaly

  • Stay hidden and grab investigators to collapse the team.
  • Use Blackout strategically to disrupt vision and pressure batteries.
  • Break line of sight and exploit map corners/rooms.

Round Rules

  • Anomaly wins by collapsing all investigators.
  • Investigators win by draining Anomaly Health.
  • If time expires, the round ends in a draw.

High-Level Technical Architecture

Host Display

  • /host runs the authoritative match simulation in-browser.
  • Displayed on laptop/TV (HDMI preferred, AirPlay fallback).

Party Server

  • Node + Socket.IO relay for room codes, lobby state, and phone input.
  • Railway-ready deployment with health and diagnostics routes.

Phone Controllers

  • /join?code=XXXX connects players to a room.
  • Sends joystick/button input; host maps input to in-game roles.
