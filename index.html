How to Play

Investigators

Track the anomaly, keep your light charged, and drain its health before the timer ends.

  • Move with WASD or arrow keys.
  • Aim with the mouse.
  • Hold left mouse to project light.
  • Controller: left stick move, right stick aim, RT light, Y or LB ability.
  • World prompts switch between keyboard and controller labels automatically.
  • Press E to pulse scan nearby hidden energy.
  • Stay close to teammates and keep light pressure on the anomaly.
  • Stand near a collapsed teammate to revive them over time.
  • Warning icons appear over investigators when the anomaly is close.
  • AI teammates revive, resupply, search, and pressure the anomaly during solo testing.
  • Use world prompts and teammate tags to read objectives quickly.
  • Host or join from the lobby browser, quick join, or an invite code.
  • Cycle Suit in the lobby to change your investigator colorway.

Anomaly

Stay hidden, collapse investigator resolve, and use the arena to break line of sight.

  • Move with WASD or arrow keys.
  • You move 15% faster than investigators.
  • Controller: left stick move, A dash, Y or LB blackout.
  • Touch investigators to instantly collapse them.
  • Press Space to dash.
  • Press E to trigger a 5-second blackout with a 30-second cooldown; investigators farther from the wave keep more battery.
  • During blackout, investigators only see through active flashlight beams.
  • Echo decoys create false signal pressure and can pull investigator aim until burned out.
  • Avoid sustained light beams.

Round Rules

Investigators win by containing the anomaly. The anomaly wins by collapsing the team.

  • Battery does not recharge on its own; one pickup starts on the map, then low battery can call in another after a 30-second cooldown.
  • When only one investigator remains active, an Overcharge battery can appear for a short comeback window.
  • Solo investigators start with extra lives for local testing.
  • Flashlights, arena events, and close contact reveal the anomaly.
  • Each arena has a timed event that can expose, distort, or misdirect the hunt.
  • Walls and lab equipment block light and pulse checks.
  • The round ends when anomaly health or tracker resolve runs out; timeout is a draw.

Accessibility

Use Motion and Contrast from the top bar to tune readability before hosting or joining.

  • Motion Reduced softens screen shake, lightning flashes, and particle bursts.
  • Contrast High brightens UI surfaces and boosts canvas readability.
